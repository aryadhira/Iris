### Screens

- **Login** (`/login`) — one passphrase field, no email, no sign-up. Wrong
  passphrase shows “Passphrase-nya belum tepat. Coba lagi.”, keeps the field
  focused, and does not clear it. Already-authenticated visits never render it.
- **Rooms** (`/rooms`) — desktop: a 292 px sidebar + a room card grid; phone:
  the same list as a sheet from the left (320 px of 390) plus an honest empty
  state. “Room baru” opens a dialog with a required name and an optional topic.
  Archive is inline and two-step: `Arsipkan <name>?` → `Ya, arsipkan` / `Batal`.
- **Room** (`/r/{id}`) — header with name, topic and a `Diarsipkan` pill;
  stored messages paint first (`GET …?limit=200`, reversed from the API's
  newest-first order); user on the right in one bubble, assistant on the left
  with a 26 px mark and no nested bubble; fenced code blocks render as `pre`
  (text nodes only, no `v-html`).
- **Composer** — autosizing textarea (168 px max), Enter sends, Shift+Enter
  newline, IME-safe. Disabled while a turn runs. Stop is rendered disabled with
  `title="Stop belum tersambung ke server…"` on a wrapper (a disabled button
  does not always show its native tooltip), plus `aria-describedby`.
- **Working state** — always text: `Bekerja · 12 detik` and a tool line
  (`Sedang berpikir`, `Sedang menjalankan perintah`, …; leading `_` stripped).
  A ticking `setInterval` drives the seconds; there is no spinner-only state.
- **Archived room** — opens read-only: the composer is disabled and a line says
  why. Verified in the browser.

### Streaming

`fetch` + `ReadableStream`; `EventSource` cannot POST. `src/lib/sse.ts` parses
`event:`/`data:` frames with a line buffer, so chunk boundaries anywhere —
inside a field name, a data line, or between `\r` and `\n` — are safe. Multiple
data lines join with `\n`; comments and unknown fields are ignored.

- `delta` appends to the streaming block, which is replaced by a real message on
  `done` with the server's `messageId`.
- After `done` the shell re-reads the mirror and merges by id.
- On a dropped connection: “Koneksi terputus — menyambung ulang”, then up to 8
  `GET /messages` attempts over ~35 s. If the stored reply appears it is adopted
  (no second POST, no duplicate); if not, the partial text is kept and labelled
  “Balasan terputus — mungkin belum lengkap.”
- The composer keeps nothing on a successful send and restores the typed text if
  the request itself failed.

`src/lib/merge.ts` is the duplicate rule: match by id first, then a local-only
message by role + body. That prevents both the optimistic user message and a
stored reply from doubling when the mirror repeats what the stream showed.

### Routing and persistence

`/` → the last opened room (`localStorage["iris:lastRoom"]`) or `/rooms`;
`/login`; `/r/{id}`. A 404 clears the remembered room and shows a way back. A
401 redirects to login with `?next=` (same-origin paths only).

### Mobile

`h-dvh` / `min-h-dvh` (not `vh`) for the shell; the composer has
`padding-bottom: max(0.75rem, env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`; the headers and
sheet respect `safe-area-inset-top`. 44 px minimum targets on the menu, send,
stop, room item, archive, new-room and login controls. Inputs are 16 px on
phones so iOS does not zoom. No horizontal overflow at 390 or 1440, measured,
including with the sheet and the dialog open.

`src/style.css` `@theme`. The only gradient is `.halo` behind the login mark.

## Screenshot findings

All screenshots are real: two rooms created through the UI, two turns answered
by Hermes, no seeded data. Files are in `changelogs/assets/`.

What looked wrong, and was fixed:

1. **Black squares around the mark.** The JPEG master has black corners; on the
   login halo and the 26 px avatar it read as a black box. PNGs now keep only
   the rounded-square area (full mark) or the circular crop (small mark).
2. **The halo was a page-wide wash.** A 440 px radial at 40 % height tinted the
   whole form lavender. It is now a 236 px glow hugging the 88 px mark.
3. **Wrong passphrase dropped focus.** The field was disabled while the check
   ran, so focus went to `body`. The field now stays enabled; focus is kept
   (measured `activeElement=passphrase`).
4. **The conversation floated at the top.** With two messages, a 1440×900 room
   had ~600 px of dead space under the reply. The column now pins to the bottom
   when the content is short and scrolls normally when it is long.
5. **The composer duplicated the just-sent text.** It held the draft until the
   backend's headers arrived, so for a second the same text sat in the bubble
   and in a disabled textarea. It clears on send and restores only on failure.
6. **A redundant “Mulai percakapan lain” row** repeated the “Room baru” button;
   removed.
7. **Test-side (not product):** the desktop sidebar stays mounted but hidden at
   phone width, so the first archive check clicked the hidden copy. The driver
   now scopes selectors to the visible sheet; the sheet itself was correct.

What still looks imperfect (and why it was left):

- At 26 px the avatar is a small violet drop with a white core; the mic arm is
  a few pixels. It is the brand's reduced mark, not a redrawn one.
- The 16 px favicon reads as a dark disc with a violet drop and a white mic
  bar. That is a real improvement over the full mark (robots/arch become noise
  at 16), but the mic detail cannot survive 16 px.
- Desktop login is one 352 px column in the middle of 1440 px — intentional and
  calm, but sparse; a two-panel brand page was out of scope.
- The desktop rooms page is a centered 768 px column with one card, matching
  the conversation column rather than a conventional left-aligned list.
- The “Hermes menjawab di sini…” reassurance line repeats under every turn.
- The archive confirm pushes the list down instead of replacing the row; it is
  clear and tap-safe, but bouncy on a short list.
- Text is 14 px body / 11.5 px quiet labels. On a bright phone outdoors the
  quiet labels are low contrast, by design of the palette.

## What is missing (deliberate)

- **Stop is not wired.** The backend has no stop endpoint yet (P3); the button
  is disabled and says so. Closing the room stops watching, not the turn.
- **No stream resume.** After a lost connection the shell only re-reads the
  mirror; it cannot re-attach to a running turn. If Hermes takes longer than
  ~35 s to store the reply, the UI asks for a reload.
- **No production static serving.** irisd does not serve `web/dist` yet; dev and
  this acceptance use Vite's proxy. That belongs to P5 (systemd/Tailscale).
- No rename, unarchive or delete. Archived rooms disappear from
  `GET /api/rooms` (backend behaviour) but still open read-only by id.
- No markdown beyond fenced code blocks, no syntax highlighting, no links.
- No PWA service worker/offline cache; the manifest and icons are in place.
- No focus trap in the dialogs (Escape and backdrop close work, focus starts in
  the right field).
- Session expiry mid-turn shows the session line in the error area but does not
  force a redirect; the next navigation does.
- Safe-area insets are implemented but cannot be measured in headless Chrome
  (insets are 0); a real notched phone and a real software keyboard still need
  a manual pass.
- Only the first 200 messages paint; “Muat pesan lama” paginates backwards.

## The one place most likely to break

The **reconnect/adopt path** in `src/stores/room.ts`. It is the only behaviour
that depends on timing rather than a protocol handshake: after the stream dies,
the shell polls `GET /messages` eight times over ~35 s and decides “the turn is
done” by finding an assistant row after the last user message. A slow Hermes, a
reply saved just after the last poll, or a proxy that buffers/breaks the SSE
stream will surface as “Koneksi terputus… muat ulang room” even though the turn
finished. It is unit-tested (adoption, no duplicate, unrecovered partial) but
not browser-tested, because losing a connection mid-turn deterministically is
not something the current harness can do without faking the network.
If a reverse proxy is ever placed in front of irisd, it must disable response
buffering for `/api/rooms/*/messages` (the daemon sets
`X-Accel-Buffering: no` for nginx; other proxies need their own switch).

## Known gaps

- **Stop is not wired** — rendered disabled with a tooltip that says so, rather than pretending.
- **Reconnect polls** `GET /messages` eight times (~35 s) to adopt a turn that finished while the tab was
  closed. Works, but it is polling; P3 should replace it with the run/event stream.
- No approvals/steering, no artifacts, no voice yet (P2–P4).
